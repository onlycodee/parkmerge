Privacy Policy — Park & Merge 3D

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PhuongVV ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Park & Merge 3D (package com.phuongvv.parkmerge, "the App").

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. We do not require an account, and we do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in this version of the App.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings (current level, coins, lives, boosters, sound/music/vibration preferences) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Permissions

The App requests only the VIBRATE permission to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. You can turn vibration off at any time in the App's Settings.

Third-party services

This version of the App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If a future version adds such services (for example ads or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing what data is collected and why.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
